== SECTION: About Al Ain University (AAU) ==

Al Ain University (AAU) is a private university in the United Arab Emirates with campuses in Al Ain and Abu Dhabi (Mohammed Bin Zayed City). AAU strives to be a leading example in providing exceptional educational services, financial responsibility, and resource management in UAE higher education, fostering an environment that supports teaching, research, and community service goals of the nation.

Vision: To be a leading university recognized for its excellence in education, research, and community service.

Mission: Foster an environment that supports the teaching, research, and community service goals of the nation.

Accreditations:
- Licensed by the UAE Ministry of Education
- QAA (UK): International Institutional Accreditation
- ABET: International accreditation for Engineering programs
- ACPE: International certification for the BSc in Pharmacy
- AACSB: Accreditation for the College of Business
- CAEP: Accreditation for specific College of Education programs
- AQAS: Accreditation for the College of Communication and Media

Accreditation bodies: UAE Ministry of Education, QAA (UK), ABET (Engineering), ACPE (Pharmacy), AACSB (Business), CAEP (Education), AQAS (Media).

== SECTION: Admissions ==

Undergraduate Admission Requirements:
- Valid UAE General Secondary School Certificate or equivalent certified by the UAE Ministry of Education
- Students from Technical stream (65%+) can apply to specific colleges (Business, Media, Education)
- Minimum GPA for transfer students: 2.0/4.0
- English Proficiency required (varies by specialization):
  * Arabic-taught programs: EmSAT 1250 / IELTS 4.5 or equivalent TOEFL
  * English-taught programs: EmSAT 1400 / IELTS 6.0 or equivalent TOEFL

Postgraduate Admission Requirements:
- Certified Bachelor's degree with minimum GPA of 2.0/4.0
- Up to 3 remedial courses may be required (4 for MBA) if prior degree does not align with the intended master's program

Required Documents for All Applicants:
- Certified High School or Bachelor's Transcript & Certificate
- Valid English Language Proficiency Certificate
- Equivalency certificate from UAE Ministry of Education (if degree issued abroad)
- Valid Passport and Emirates ID
- Two passport-sized photographs
- Health Certificate

Application Process:
- Applications are accepted for Fall (August) and Spring (January) semesters
- Apply online at apply.aau.ac.ae
- Contact admissions at +800-22864 for specific deadline dates

== SECTION: Student Portal and Course Registration ==

AAU uses two main digital platforms for students:
1. Student Self-Service Portal (student.aau.ac.ae) - for registration, schedules, grades, financial info
2. Moodle LMS - for online courses, submitting assignments, conducting online exams, and accessing course materials

How to Register for Courses:
1. Login to the student portal using your AAU Student ID and password
2. Navigate to the "Register for Classes" tab
3. Select the upcoming semester from the drop-down menu
4. Search for available courses using specific criteria or Course Reference Number (CRN)
5. Add desired courses and click "Submit" to officially register
Note: The system automatically blocks registration if prerequisites are not met or if there are campus/major restrictions (shown as red error messages)

Add/Drop Process:
- The Add/Drop period occurs during the first week of each semester
- During Add/Drop: Dropping a course results in 100% refund with no academic penalty
- Process: In the registration portal, select the course, choose "Remove" from the drop-down, click "Submit"

Withdrawal Policy (after Add/Drop period closes):
- Dropping a course after the Add/Drop window results in financial penalties (40% to 100% fine)
- The course will appear as "W" (Withdrawal) on the student's transcript


== SECTION: Grading System ==

AAU uses a 4.0 GPA scale for all programs.

Undergraduate Grading Scale:
- A (90–100%): 4.0 GPA points — Excellent
- B+ (85–89%): 3.5 GPA points
- B (80–84%): 3.0 GPA points — Very Good
- C+ (75–79%): 2.5 GPA points
- C (70–74%): 2.0 GPA points — Satisfactory
- D+ (65–69%): 1.5 GPA points
- D (60–64%): 1.0 GPA point
- F (below 60%): 0 GPA points — Fail
- Minimum passing grade: D (60%)

Postgraduate (Master's) Grading Scale:
- A (90–100%): 4.0 GPA points
- B+ (85–89%): 3.5 GPA points
- B (80–84%): 3.0 GPA points
- C+ (75–79%): 2.5 GPA points
- C (70–74%): 2.0 GPA points
- F (below 70%): 0 GPA points — Fail
- Minimum passing grade: C (70%)

Special Grade Notations:
- FA: Fail due to Absence
- I: Incomplete (makeup exam pending, must be resolved within the following semester)
- W: Withdrawal (recorded when dropping after the Add/Drop period)


== SECTION: Academic Policies ==

Attendance Policy:
- 10% Absence from a course: First Warning issued
- 20% Absence from a course: Second Warning issued
- 25% Absence from a course: Automatic failure — grade of FA (Fail due to Absence) is recorded

Makeup Exam Policy (Incomplete Grades):
- If a student misses a final exam due to a documented emergency:
  * Submit an "Incomplete" application form to the Dean within 5 working days of the missed exam
  * Attach a valid excuse document (medical report, official letter, etc.)
  * Must have completed at least 36% of coursework/midterm grades to qualify
  * The makeup final exam must be completed by the end of the second week of the following semester
  * If makeup exam is not taken, the "I" grade automatically converts to "F"

Academic Probation — Undergraduate:
- Probation is triggered when Cumulative GPA (CGPA) drops below 2.0
- 1st Warning: Academic load restricted to a maximum of 15 credit hours the next semester
- 2nd Warning: Load restricted to 12 credit hours if CGPA remains below 2.0
- 3rd Warning: Load restricted to 9 credit hours; continued failure may result in forced major/college transfer or academic dismissal

Academic Probation — Postgraduate (Master's):
- Warning triggered when CGPA drops below 3.0 (after the first semester)
- 1st Warning: Credit load restricted to 9 credit hours
- 2nd Warning: Load restricted to 6 credit hours; failure to raise CGPA to 3.0 may result in dismissal

== SECTION: International Students ==

Specific Requirements for Non-UAE Students:
- Equivalency Certificate: International academic degrees or high school diplomas must be officially equated by the UAE Ministry of Education
- Language Proficiency:
  * Undergraduates: IELTS 4.5–5.0 (or TOEFL/EmSAT equivalents)
  * Postgraduates: IELTS 5.0–6.0 depending on program and language of instruction
- Required documents: Valid passport copy, health certificate, passport-sized photographs, certified transcripts

Visa Process for International Students:
1. Receive official acceptance letter from AAU and pay admission/registration fees
2. AAU provides a visa sponsorship letter (AAU acts as the student's sponsor)
3. AAU assists with initiating the visa process with UAE authorities

Additional Visa Requirements:
- UAE medical fitness test (blood test and chest X-ray)
- Proof of adequate financial means to support studies
- Mandatory UAE medical health insurance (strictly enforced in Abu Dhabi)
